// 01 — Know Your Enemy

Common Cyber Threats

Understanding what attackers use is the first step to defending yourself. Here are the most common threats you need to know.

🎣
Phishing
Fake emails or websites that trick you into giving your password, OTP, or bank details. Most common attack method worldwide.
HIGH RISK
🦠
Malware
Malicious software — viruses, trojans, spyware — that secretly installs on your device and steals data or damages files.
HIGH RISK
🔐
Ransomware
Hackers lock your files and demand money (ransom) to unlock them. Has crippled hospitals, banks, and governments worldwide.
CRITICAL
👤
Social Engineering
Manipulating people psychologically — pretending to be IT support, a friend, or bank staff — to get sensitive information.
MEDIUM RISK
📡
Man-in-the-Middle
Attacker secretly intercepts communication between two parties — like reading your messages or stealing login sessions on public WiFi.
TECHNICAL
💉
SQL Injection
Hackers inject malicious code into website forms to access, modify, or delete entire databases. A major web vulnerability.
DEV RISK
// 02 — Stay Safe

10 Rules to Protect Yourself

Simple habits that make you 90% harder to hack. Follow these and you are already safer than most people online.

01

Use Strong Passwords

Minimum 12 characters. Mix uppercase, numbers, symbols. Never use your name or birthday.

02

Enable 2FA Everywhere

Two-Factor Authentication adds a second lock. Even if your password leaks, hackers can't get in.

03

Never Click Unknown Links

Hover over links to check the real URL. If it looks suspicious — delete it. Banks never ask for OTP over email.

04

Keep Software Updated

Updates patch security vulnerabilities. Outdated apps are easy targets for hackers.

05

Use a VPN on Public WiFi

Public networks are dangerous. VPN encrypts your traffic so no one can intercept it.

06

Back Up Your Data

Use 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, 2 different formats, 1 offsite/cloud. Ransomware can't hurt you if you have backups.

07

Use a Password Manager

Tools like Bitwarden or LastPass generate and store complex passwords so you don't have to remember them.

08

Check HTTPS on Websites

Always look for the padlock icon before entering any personal or payment information online.

09

Limit Social Media Info

Hackers use your public birthday, city, and school to guess passwords or answer security questions.

10

Report Suspicious Activity

In India, report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. Early reporting helps stop further damage.
